Internal message · Marked for management review

Please do not fill every ‘empty’ slot automatically

FromDr. Marcus Hill · Medical Director, Outpatient ServicesToPatient Access Improvement TeamDateNovember 9, 2026 · 8:41 AM

I heard a proposal to use an AI scheduler to place waiting patients into any appointment block that appears open. I understand the frustration behind that idea. Patients are waiting, phones keep ringing, and an unused-looking block can seem like wasted capacity.

But some blocks are held for specific follow-ups or visit types. A short routine visit is not interchangeable with a complex appointment. If software fills every visible opening without understanding those clinical boundaries, the calendar may look better while patients and clinicians face new risks. We must not confuse an administrative opening with a clinically appropriate appointment.

I am not defending every old scheduling rule. Some of our rules may be outdated. I am asking that we identify the actual failure before automating a decision with clinical consequences.

Re: Please do not fill every ‘empty’ slot automatically

FromKaren Blake · Patient Experience & Communications LeadToPatient Access Improvement TeamDateNovember 9, 2026 · 9:16 AM

I agree that clinical judgment matters. But many patients never get a clear explanation of why a slot cannot be offered, who owns their referral, or when someone will call back. A safe system that people cannot navigate is not automatically a fair one. Please include patient access and communication in this review.
