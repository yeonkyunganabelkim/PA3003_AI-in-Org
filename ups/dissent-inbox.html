A ‘resolved’ ticket that resolved nothing

FromLaura Bennett · Customer Solutions ManagerToOperations Management ReviewDateNovember 9, 2026 · 7:52 AM

Another customer called after seeing DELIVERED on the tracking page. Our agent found a completed scan and sent the approved response asking the customer to check nearby entrances. The inquiry was then closed according to procedure. Less than an hour later, the customer contacted us again: no package, no explanation, and no one able to say which door had actually been used.

At our last meeting, I heard suggestions that the drivers simply need more monitoring. Maybe some scan practices deserve review. But my agents cannot reopen a delivery event or verify every doorstep handoff, and our closure procedure does not consistently record whether the customer ever received the parcel. Staff are uncomfortable saying this aloud because it sounds as though they are questioning the center’s service reports.

If our system says DELIVERED and our support system says RESOLVED, why is the customer still searching?
