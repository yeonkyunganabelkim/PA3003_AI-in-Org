The meal arrived; the message never did

FromHannah Seo · Recipient Relations CoordinatorToService Quality Review TeamDateNovember 9, 2026 · 9:28 AM

A volunteer finished a delivery and heard a recipient ask whether someone could call about an additional service. The delivery was recorded as completed. The volunteer believed the note would reach the appropriate coordinator. Later, recipient relations could not find a clear follow-up request in the usual workflow.

Everyone involved may have followed the steps they understood. The problem is that there are several places to record a concern, and not every type of note has a clearly assigned next person. We are discussing ways to make routes more efficient, but saving minutes will not help if important requests disappear between a doorstep and an office.

I want us to remember that successful meal delivery and successful follow-up are not the same task. A completed stop should not make a person's request invisible.
